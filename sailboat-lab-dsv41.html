Lift & drag coefficient model

Angle of attack. The boom angle τ is measured from the centreline (+ = boom to starboard). The apparent wind angle β is signed from the bow (+ = wind from starboard). The signed sail AoA is δ = β + τ (wrapped to ±180°). Sheet the boom to leeward (τ opposite in sign to β) and |δ| = |β| − |τ|; sheet it to windward and |δ| > 90° — the sail is backwinded and luffs.

Attached flow (|δ| ≤ 13°). CL = 6.4·|δ| per radian, capped at 1.45. Small-angle lift is linear and continuous through zero.

Approaching stall (13–27°). CL falls linearly from 1.45 to 0.55; CD rises steeply as the boundary layer separates.

Stalled (27–90°). CL decays to zero at broadside; CD rises to a flat-plate maximum of 2.0 near 90°. This is the regime that drives a boat dead downwind.

Backwinded (|δ| > 90°). No lift. The sail is a bluff plate: F = q·A·CDp·(d̂·n̂)·n̂ along the sail normal, with CDp = 1.15 + 0.8·sin²(180−|δ|). This is the force that lets a backed sail shove the bow out of irons.

Directions. Drag acts along the relative air velocity d̂; lift is perpendicular, l̂ = sign(δ)·perp(d̂) — the same rule on both tacks. q = ½·ρ·AWS². Bounded and continuous at zero wind: q vanishes, directions stay finite.

Not CFD. A plausible single-sail envelope for an exhibit, not measured polar data.

Water forces, heel & conventions

Frames. World: +X east, −Z north, +Y up; heading ψ clockwise from north. Body: u forward, v starboard, yaw rate ω positive to starboard, heel φ positive to starboard. Trim τ positive to starboard; rudder positive turns the bow to starboard.

Apparent wind. v_app = v_true − v_CE, where the centre-of-effort velocity includes the boat's translation and ω × r (the stern swings opposite the bow).

Hull. Quadratic surge resistance plus a steep wave-making wall above hull speed (3.4 m/s) so the boat cannot be driven faster than the wind that drives it. Lateral resistance is a separate quadratic term.

Keel & rudder. Symmetric foils. Local water velocity at offset (a,b) is (u − ωb, v + ωa). AoA is the signed angle between the local flow and the foil chord; lift is perpendicular to the local flow, drag opposes it. The rudder chord rotates with the rudder angle, so a centred rudder is a symmetric foil and a deflected one produces a stern force. No water flow ⇒ exactly zero force.

Heel. I_roll·φ̈ = M_heel − m·g·GM·sin φ − C_roll·φ̇, GM = 0.62 m, valid to ±38°. The sim pauses at 40° rather than pretending to model a capsize.

Integration. Fixed 1/240 s semi-implicit Euler substeps in an accumulator; at most 0.25 s of catch-up per rendered frame, so a backgrounded tab cannot explode the state. Physics is independent of render frame rate.

Airflow. Particles advect through an analytic illustrative field (free-stream + sail deflection + stall-scaled wake). They visualise the aerodynamic state; they never compute the forces.
