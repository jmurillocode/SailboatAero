Coefficient model
Signed angle of attack α is measured from sail chord (luff → leech) to apparent air velocity. Lift is perpendicular to that velocity; drag follows it. The model is symmetric on port and starboard.

q = ½ρV² · L = qS CL · D = qS CD

Lift rises smoothly to CL = 1.25 at 28°, then progressively washes out to zero at 90°. Drag combines profile, induced and broadside terms: 0.07 + 1.55 sin²α + 0.18 CL². Keel and rudder use bounded symmetric foil curves with earlier stall.

This is a reduced-order engineering model, not CFD. Water is still; hull drag opposes water-relative motion. Heel uses mgGM sin φ within ±35°, with a pause at 60°.

Coordinates & assumptions
Model axes: +x forward, +y starboard, +z up. World angles increase counter-clockwise from +x. Positive yaw is port; positive rudder commands starboard. Heel is signed roll about +x.

Still water, rigid sails, no waves in the force balance, no added mass, no heel-dependent wetted area, and uniform flow over each foil. The decorative water shader never feeds energy into dynamics.
